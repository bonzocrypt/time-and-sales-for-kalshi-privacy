Delete your data

Developer: Daniel Belliveau

You can request deletion of personal information held by the developer without installing the app or signing in. This page also explains how to remove information stored only on your phone.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email bonzocrypt@gmail.com with the subject Time & Sales for Kalshi - Data deletion request.
  2. Say that you want your support correspondence and associated personal information deleted. If possible, send the request from the email address you previously used for support, and identify any relevant conversation dates.
  3. Requests are completed within 30 days of receipt, except for information that must be retained by law. You will receive a response explaining completion or any required retention.

Never send your API key, private key, password, or financial-account details. Sending this request does not require access to your Kalshi account.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, send an email manually to the address above.

What the developer deletes

On request, I delete support emails and attachments under my control, including the email address, name, and other personal information contained in that correspondence. I do not operate a server that stores your Kalshi private key, watchlist, or signal history.

There is no additional routine retention period after the 30-day request window for correspondence covered by the request. If a legal obligation requires retaining specific information, only that information is kept for the legally required period. The response will explain the reason and applicable period, unless disclosure is prohibited by law.

Remove information stored on your phone

  • API credentials only: Open the app's Settings and select Clear to remove the saved API Key ID and private key. This does not erase your local history or revoke the key at Kalshi.
  • All local app data: In Android Settings, go to Apps > Time & Sales for Kalshi > Storage > Clear storage. Device wording may vary. This removes saved credentials, watchlists, preferences, alert settings, downloaded market records, and signal observations and outcomes.
  • Backups: Manage any device or cloud backups separately through your backup provider's settings.

Local data remains on your device until removed; it is not automatically deleted within 90 days. I cannot remotely erase records stored only on your device.

What this request does not delete

  • Your Kalshi account and Kalshi's records: Contact Kalshi directly for account or data requests. To revoke an API key, use your Kalshi account settings. Clearing this app does not close that account or revoke the key.
  • Service-provider records: Kalshi, Expo, Google, and GitHub may retain request, update, diagnostic, security, or website-access records under their own policies. Those records are not automatically erased by clearing the app or deleting support correspondence. Their retention periods are set by the providers, not by this app's 30-day support-data deletion commitment.

For provider privacy links and the full explanation of data handling, see the Privacy Policy. This app does not create a separate developer-hosted user account.
