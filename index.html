Time & Sales for Kalshi

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Time & Sales for Kalshi is developed and provided by Daniel Belliveau ("I," "me," or "the developer"). This policy explains how the Android app and this policy webpage handle information. For privacy questions or requests, email bonzocrypt@gmail.com.

The app displays market data and performs read-only analysis. Your Kalshi private key is stored securely on your device and used there to sign requests. Market history, watchlists, and signal results are stored locally. The app connects to Kalshi for market data and to Expo for app updates.

Request deletion of your data — email steps, the 30-day request timeframe, and instructions for clearing local app data.

1. Kalshi credentials and connections

You can choose public market access without an account or API key. This mode requests real market data periodically from Kalshi without attaching credentials. Kalshi still receives your IP address and the market identifiers and request details needed to provide that data. A separate, clearly labeled demo generates simulated market data locally.

For optional streaming access, you may enter your own Kalshi API Key ID and RSA private key. Use a key with read-only permissions. The app saves these credentials using the device's secure storage and loads the private key into memory to authenticate requests. The public-data feed does not use saved credentials. The private key itself is not sent to Kalshi, Expo, or the developer.

Authenticated requests sent directly to Kalshi include your API Key ID, a timestamp, and a digital signature. Kalshi also receives your IP address and the market identifiers or other request details needed to provide the data you request. These connections use HTTPS or secure WebSockets. Kalshi can associate authenticated requests with your Kalshi account.

The app requests market information, public trade prints, order books, and available event context. It does not request your portfolio, personal order history, balance, bank information, identity documents, or Kalshi password. It does not place orders or transfer money. Kalshi handles information it receives under its own privacy policy.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores downloaded market data, public trade prints, order-book snapshots, saved markets and watchlist details, preferences, alert settings, and signal observations and outcomes in its local database. This information supports the tape, watchlist, alerts, and historical analysis. These records are not uploaded to a developer-operated database or synchronized between your devices by the app.

The app may write diagnostic messages to device logs to help investigate errors. Its logging code masks private-key fields. Do not include credentials in screenshots or support messages. Device or platform diagnostic and backup services may handle app information according to your device settings and their own policies.

3. App updates and service providers

The app uses Expo's EAS Update service to check for and download compatible app updates. Update requests send technical information including your IP address, operating-system platform, app runtime/version and update identifiers, and a randomly generated installation identifier. Failed-update identifiers and technical startup-error information may also be sent to diagnose or recover from update failures. This information supports update delivery and reliability. The app does not attach your Kalshi credentials, watchlist, or signal history to these requests. See Expo's privacy policy.

Google Play handles installation, updates distributed through the store, and applicable installer-protection checks. Google may process installation, device, and diagnostic information under its privacy policy and your Google/device settings.

If you email support, Google/Gmail processes your email as the email service provider. If you open an external link, the destination website receives the normal information associated with a web visit and applies its own privacy policy.

4. Notifications, permissions, and advertising

If you enable signal alerts and grant notification permission, the app can generate notifications locally on your device. The current app does not register for Expo's remote push-notification service. You can disable alerts in the app or turn off its notifications in Android settings. Notification content can appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location. It contains no advertising SDK or advertising tracking and does not sell or rent your personal information. There is no separate behavioral-analytics SDK; the update-related technical information described above is still processed by Expo.

5. Support and privacy requests

If you contact bonzocrypt@gmail.com, I receive your email address, any name you provide, and the content and attachments you choose to send. I use them to respond, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy requests. Please do not send API keys, private keys, passwords, or financial-account details.

I do not share support correspondence for advertising. Service providers may process it to deliver email and support. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or protect legal rights.

6. Retention and removal

  • Saved credentials: In the app, open Settings and use Clear to remove the saved API Key ID and private key. This does not delete the local market history or revoke the key at Kalshi. Revoke the key separately in your Kalshi account to invalidate it.
  • Local records: The app retains its local database between sessions; it does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period. On Android, open the device's Settings, select this app under Apps, and use Storage > Clear storage (the wording varies by device) to remove its local data and credentials. Uninstalling also removes the app's local storage. Any device backups must be managed separately through your backup provider and settings.
  • Support correspondence: I retain correspondence as needed to handle the request and related support, security, or legal obligations. You may request deletion by email. Requests to delete support correspondence and attachments under my control are completed within 30 days of receipt, except information legally required to be retained. There is no additional routine retention period for correspondence covered by the request after that window. If legal retention is required, the response will explain the reason and applicable period unless disclosure is prohibited by law.
  • Provider records: Kalshi, Expo, Google, and GitHub control retention of the service records they receive under their own policies. Removing app data does not automatically remove those records.

The app does not create a separate developer-hosted account. Removing the app or its credentials does not close your Kalshi account. Contact Kalshi directly about that account. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. Contact me to exercise applicable rights concerning information I hold. I cannot remotely access or delete records held only on your device.

7. Security and processing locations

Credentials are protected using device secure storage; network requests use encrypted connections. The market-history database is stored in the app's local storage, rather than the credential keystore. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and revoke a Kalshi key if you believe it has been exposed.

The service providers described here may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate, subject to their policies and applicable law.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact me so I can address it.

9. This webpage

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security and processes other technical information under its privacy statement. This page adds no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or submission forms.

10. Changes and contact
